## **1. Introduction** 

## **1.1 Purpose** 

The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive **Software Requirements Specification (SRS)** for the **Smart Department** —a web-based platform designed to digitize and streamline the academic operations of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at **Jahangirnagar University, Savar, Dhaka-1342, Bangladesh** . 

Currently, the department manages its academic scheduling, classroom allocation, class tracking, Class Test (CT) scheduling, result management, and resource distribution through manual or semi-manual processes. These traditional methods often lead to scheduling conflicts, inefficient resource utilization, communication gaps between faculty members and students, and the absence of a centralized academic information management system. 

The proposed system aims to address these challenges by providing a unified digital platform with role-based access control. Through this platform: 

- Administrators can allocate classrooms and laboratories for the entire semester while avoiding scheduling conflicts. 

- Teachers can manage class schedules, attendance, class tests, assignments, and assessment records. 

- Class Representatives (CRs) can upload academic resources, notices, and class test results for students. 

- Students can access their personalized class schedules, classroom information, examination results, CT marks, and downloadable study materials from a single portal. 

The system is designed to improve academic coordination, enhance communication among stakeholders, optimize resource utilization, and ensure efficient management of

## **2.1 User Classes and Characteristics** 

The system defines four distinct user classes, each with different access levels, responsibilities, and interaction patterns. All users belong to the CSE department of Jahangirnagar University. 

### **2.1.1 Normal Student** 

#### **Description:** 

Normal Students are the primary consumers of the system. They are undergraduate (Honours) or postgraduate (Masters) students currently enrolled in the CSE department. Each student is associated with a specific batch and a current semester. The system provides them with a personalized view of their academic schedule, upcoming assessments, marks, and downloadable resources. 

Due to the nature of the department’s academic progression, not all students follow a linear path through their semesters. The system must accommodate the following student sub-categories, all managed by the Admin:

### **2.1.3 Teacher** 

#### **Description:** 

Teachers are faculty members of the CSE department who use the system to manage their classes, assessments, and academic events. A teacher may be involved with multiple batches across different semesters simultaneously. Each teacher is assigned a unique system ID to ensure accountability and prevent unauthorized modification of other teachers’ schedules. 

The department has a **Chairman** role, which is held by one teacher at a time. The Chairman is a teacher with elevated administrative privileges (e.g., allocating seminars and workshops). The Chairman role is **rotational** — when a teacher’s tenure as Chairman ends, they transition back to a regular Teacher role, and another teacher is assigned as Chairman. The Admin manages this role transition within the system.

## **2.4 Constraints** 

### **2.4.1 Design and Implementation Constraints** 

This section documents the conditions, standards, and policies that bound all design and implementation choices for Smart Department . Every technical decision must remain within the limits described below. 

#### **2.4.1.1 Regulatory and Legal Constraints** 

Smart Department stores and processes personal data of students, teachers, and administrative staff of a public university. Compliance with the following legal frameworks is **mandatory** : 

- **Bangladesh ICT Act, 2006** (amended 2009, 2013) – governs electronic records, digital transactions, and unauthorised access to computer systems. The system must not store, transmit, or process data in ways that violate this Act. 

- **Bangladesh Digital Security Act, 2018** – regulates digital content, cybercrime, and data privacy in Bangladesh. Any feature involving public data disclosure or inter-system data transfer must be evaluated against this Act before implementation. 

- **Jahangirnagar University IT Usage Policies** – the system must conform to JU’s internal policies on acceptable use, data handling, and IT infrastructure. Deployment on JU servers requires prior approval from the JU IT Division.

#### **2.4.1.2 University and Departmental Policy Constraints** 

- The system must reflect the CSE Department’s academic structure: **Honors** (8 semesters, 4 years) and **Masters** (2 semesters, 1 year), with each semester lasting approximately **6 months** and two semesters per academic year.
